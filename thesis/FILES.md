# LIST OF FILES

## main.tex
The start of a new thesis.

## main.bib
The bibliography for it.

## palthesis.tex
A short example thesis, which approximately the same contents as example palindrome2.tex elsewhere – see https://www.overleaf.com/read/hfqxgcjssvxx .
Not need for your thesis.

## pal.bib, pal.py
pal.bib is the thesis bibliography (copied from Overleaf); pal.py is used by palthesis.tex and is not needed for the thesis.

## cluu.cls
The definition of the CLUU class.

## cluu.bbx, cluu.cbx
Needed for the CLUU style for bibliographies.

## UU_*.pdf
Images needed for the cover of the theses.

## main_changes.tex
The thesis with every passage added or changed since the defended version highlighted in
yellow (deleted text is not shown). Generated with latexdiff from the defended version
(commit 5fc0390) and the current main.tex; tables are shown in their new form, with changed
captions highlighted. Compile with LuaLaTeX (it uses lua-ul); with other engines it compiles
without highlighting. Regenerate after further edits rather than editing it by hand.
